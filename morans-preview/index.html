The Clarenbridge bed

Where the river meets the Atlantic.

The Clarenbridge oyster bed lies at the mouths of the Dunkellin and Clarenbridge rivers. Oysters need a mix of fresh and sea water, and this bed has both.

It is a natural bed. It is not cultivated in any way.

Inside the cottage

An open fire, a timber ceiling and a family welcome.

Moran’s is a family business that dates back more than 250 years. In the 1800s it did a flourishing trade, when the Weir was a busy little port. The story continues today.
